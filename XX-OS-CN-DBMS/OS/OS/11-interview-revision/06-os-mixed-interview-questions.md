# OS Mixed Interview Drill

Answer these aloud without looking at notes:

1. Walk from `main()` to a disk read.
2. Explain a context switch to a non-technical interviewer.
3. A service has 1,000 threads and poor latency. What OS-level questions do you ask?
4. A process uses 8 GB virtual memory but 1 GB RSS. How is that possible?
5. Why does a TLB miss not necessarily cause a page fault?
6. How can a mutex create a deadlock?
7. Why can high CPU utilization still mean a service is unhealthy?
8. What happens to child processes when a parent exits?
9. Why can event-driven servers handle many idle connections efficiently?
10. What is the difference between a container and a VM?
11. Why can swapping destroy latency?
12. Why can too-small Round Robin quanta hurt throughput?
13. How would you diagnose file-descriptor exhaustion?
14. Why is shared memory fast but dangerous?
15. Why can a deleted log file still consume disk space?
16. Why is LRU difficult to implement exactly at hardware scale?
17. How does a page become dirty?
18. Why does DMA reduce CPU overhead?
19. How does lock ordering prevent circular wait?
20. What does least privilege mean for a backend service?
